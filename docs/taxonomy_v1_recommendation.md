## Notes for Phase 1

- The taxonomy should separate service area from urgency/sensitivity. For example, a transportation request may be routine, but a roadway hazard may need escalation.
- Public safety and CSB/mental health should be included only with strong escalation language, low autonomy, and clear emergency/non-emergency boundaries.
- HHS/basic needs should route to Coordinated Services Planning and official program pages, not make eligibility judgments.
- PLUS should be used as an approved routing destination, but dynamic PLUS forms should be reviewed before static ingestion.
- The source inventory should add any performance-measure PDFs or dashboards used as evidence during taxonomy validation.

## Open Questions

- Should Parks and Recreation be included in v1 for broader resident-service coverage, or held for v1.1?
- Should public health be grouped under HHS or kept as a separate service category?
- Should mental health / CSB be included in the public-facing taxonomy slice, or represented only as an escalation/sensitive-topic route?
- Should Voting and Elections be deferred unless the pilot is evaluated near an election cycle?
