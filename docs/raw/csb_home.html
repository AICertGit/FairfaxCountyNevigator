What We Do

The Fairfax-Falls Church Community Services Board (CSB) provides lifesaving services for people experiencing mental health concerns, substance misuse and/or developmental disabilities. Our vision is that everyone in our community has the support needed to live a healthy, fulfilling life.

Mental Health

Counseling, medication, crisis response, and other services for people of all ages with mental health concerns.

  • Learn More

Developmental Disabilities

Services to help people with developmental disabilities live, work and take part in our community.

  • Learn More

Substance Use Disorder

Treatment, detoxification, recovery supports for people with substance use disorders.

  • Learn More

Children, Youth and Families

Counseling, medication, crisis response, recovery supports and other services for children and youth (and their families).

  • Learn More

Prevention and Wellness

Assists the community in strengthening its emotional health and ability to handle substance misuse & mental illness challenges.

  • Learn More

Detoxification

Services to help individuals safely detoxify from the effects of drugs and/or alcohol.

About the Health & Human Services System

The Community Services Board is a part of the Fairfax County Health & Human Services System (HHS). The HHS System is a network of county agencies and community partners that support the well-being of all who live, work, and play in Fairfax County.
